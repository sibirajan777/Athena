## Project: Flip AI
Flip AI is an e-commerce trust product concept targeting Flipkart's fake review and delivery problems. The core features are fake review detection, review summarization, delivery delay prediction, return fraud risk scoring, and AI customer support. I used a P0/P1/P2 framework to prioritize features, with AI customer support and personalized recommendations marked as P2 (cut for MVP).

## Project: Sentra
Sentra is a hybrid ML and LLM cybersecurity anomaly detection prototype. It fuses Isolation Forest anomaly scoring with a Claude API reasoning layer to explain why an anomaly was flagged. It was built for the FinSpark'26 hackathon hosted by Bank of Maharashtra, targeting Problem Statement 2 on correlating cybersecurity telemetry with transactional behavior.
